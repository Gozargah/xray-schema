UDP FinalMask layers are applied in order, with the first item being the innermost camouflage layer.

Used together with `raw` UDP, `kcp`, `hysteria`, `xhttp` H3, and `masque` HTTP/3.

Supported UDP layer types:

- `header-custom`
- `header-dns`
- `header-dtls`
- `header-srtp`
- `header-utp`
- `header-wechat`
- `header-wireguard`
- `mkcp-original`
- `mkcp-aes128gcm`
- `noise`
- `salamander`
- `sudoku`
- `xdns`
- `xicmp`
- `realm`
- `udphop`
- `filter`
